# StoryForge Studio — Permanent Video Production & Cinematic Quality Standards

These standards apply permanently to EVERY video project, script, audio render, and visual composition in this application.

## 1. General Video Quality
- Every video must feel like a real, professionally produced video scene from a drama or film.
- Never produce static slideshows, basic pans over unrelated stock, or game-like visuals.
- Multi-angle camera direction: wide establishing, over-the-shoulder, medium shots, and tight emotional close-ups.
- Consistent visual characters: face, hair, clothing, lighting, and environment must remain consistent across cuts.

## 2. Spoken Dialogue Integrity
- Spoken audio must contain ONLY the words the characters actually speak.
- NEVER voice stage directions, visual descriptions, camera instructions, or parentheticals (e.g. `(hesitates)`, `[Looking away]`, `Ryan sighs`).
- Stage directions and parentheticals must be parsed into visual actions and camera cues, not spoken TTS.

## 3. Storytelling & Script Progression
- Clear emotional progression: **Suspicion → Confrontation → Denial → Evidence → Emotional Reaction → Deeper Questions → Tension → Partial Admission → Unresolved Ending**.
- Each line must add new information or emotion. No circular repetition.
- Characters react directly to what the other person just said.

## 4. Natural Human Voice Performance
- Natural conversational rhythm, human breathing, realistic hesitation (`I— I didn't...`, `Wait...`).
- Subtle rate and pitch adjustments that match emotional delivery.
- Dynamic pauses between speakers; avoid mechanical, robotic pacing.

## 5. Clean Audio Subsystem
- Zero static, zero buzzing, zero hiss, zero crackling, zero procedural oscillator synth noise.
- Background music must be clean, instrumental, cinematic, and royalty-free.
- Sidechain auto-ducking must duck music during dialogue so spoken words remain crystal clear.

## 6. Visual Consistency & Dynamic Camera
- Characters must look like the same people from shot to shot.
- Camera movements must serve the story (slow dolly push, organic handheld drift, depth-of-field focus pull).
- No unnatural AI glitches or frozen states.
